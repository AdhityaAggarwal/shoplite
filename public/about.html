About ShopLite

A simple project to demonstrate a clean, modern shopping site layout.

Our approach

We keep the design minimal, fast, and easy to understand. The goal is to present products in a straightforward way and provide a smooth browsing experience.

Tech stack

Built with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and a Node.js/Express server. No database or advanced security since it's a college demo.
